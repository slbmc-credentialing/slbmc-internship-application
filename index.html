About the internship programme

What is the internship programme?

The Internship Programme is a rigorous one-year training experience designed to give you a solid foundation in clinical medicine. It is structured to prepare you for full licensure and independent general practice. Without the successful completion of the Internship Programme, you cannot be fully registered as a doctor or medical practitioner in Antigua and Barbuda.

Rotations and training

🏥

Core rotations

Interns rotate through all major clinical departments, gaining broad-based clinical experience under close supervision.

Internal Medicine Paediatrics Obstetrics & Gynaecology General Surgery
📚

Six core competencies

The curriculum for each rotation is built around six competencies that guide your development as a practitioner:

Patient Care Medical Knowledge Practice-Based Learning Professionalism Communication Skills Systems-Based Practice
👥

Supervised learning environment

Your training will be led and supervised by Consultant Faculty dedicated to house staff training. You will benefit from problem-based learning, bedside teaching, and case presentations. Frequent feedback and formal evaluations are built into the programme.

🕐

Commitment and expectations

Interns function as full physicians within the hospital, taking calls on a one-in-four basis. SLBMC is committed to providing the highest quality care in a compassionate environment, and all interns are expected to reflect this standard in their conduct and professional presentation.

Professional standards

What will be expected of you

To succeed in the programme, you will need to demonstrate personal accountability, professionalism, and a genuine commitment to learning. Interns are expected to:

  • Make the care of your patient your first concern at all times
  • Recognise the limits of your own competence and seek guidance when needed
  • Maintain accurate and clear clinical records that can be understood by colleagues
  • Respect patient confidentiality and dignity in all interactions
  • Work effectively as part of a multidisciplinary team
  • Take increasing responsibility for patient care under appropriate supervision
  • Engage in self-directed learning alongside structured teaching activities
  • Demonstrate the principles of infection control, risk management, and safe prescribing

Why complete your internship at SLBMC?

SLBMC is Antigua and Barbuda’s national hospital, offering a breadth of clinical experience that supports both your professional development and your path to full medical registration. Our Consultant Faculty is dedicated to house staff training, and the programme’s structured approach ensures you graduate with the competencies required for independent practice.

How to apply — step by step

⚠️ Application period: 01 April – 30 June. Submit by 30 June for September intake consideration.
1

Read about the programme

Before applying, familiarise yourself with the programme overview, rotations, and what will be expected of you during the internship year.

About the programme →

Review the requirements

Read the full requirements list carefully. Every document must meet the specified validity and language standards before submission.

View requirements →
3

Complete the application form

Open the SLBMC Clinical Privilege Application Form and fill in all required sections. Ensure all information is accurate and complete before signing. You can validate and download it as a PDF directly from the form.

Open application form →
4

Gather your supporting documents

Collect all required documents including your diploma, transcripts, reference letters, police record, and immunisation record. Use the requirements checklist to ensure nothing is missed.

⚠ Reference letters and police records must not be older than 3 months at the time of submission.
5

Arrange official transcript transmission

Your original transcripts must be sent directly from your medical school to SLBMC — by email or by mail — unless the transcripts are duly notarised.

⚠ Do not submit transcripts yourself unless they are notarised.
6

Arrange your reference letters

Three (3) reference letters are required. Letters should be sent directly from the referee to SLBMC, addressed to the Medical Director. A signed copy including the referee’s telephone number may be accepted from the applicant. Unsigned letters will not be accepted under any circumstances.

7

Verify language and certification requirements

All documents must be in English. If any original document is not in English, submit both the original and a certified English translation. Certified or notarised copies of diplomas, certificates, or transcripts must clearly bear the notation “originals seen.”

8

Submit your application

The quickest way to submit is online — attach your documents directly on the Requirements page and click “Submit Application Package.” If you prefer, you may instead send your complete package by email to clinical.privileges@msjmc.org, or by mail/courier or hand delivery to the Information Desk at the hospital. Please include your full name and “Internship Application” in the subject line of any email.

Frequently asked questions

Application process
When does the application period open and close? +
The application period opens on 01 April and closes on 30 June each year. Applications submitted by 30 June will receive full consideration for the September intake.
When is the internship intake? +
The internship intake takes place in September each year. To be considered, your complete application must be received by 30 June.
When will I know if I have been shortlisted? +
Interviews are generally scheduled from the third week of July. All applicants are notified of their application status by email. Please allow until the third week of July before following up on your application.
What email address should I use to submit my application? +
All application documents and reference letters should be sent to clinical.privileges@msjmc.org. Please include your full name and “Internship Application” in the subject line.
How do I submit my application? +
The quickest way is to attach your documents directly on the Requirements page of this website and submit them online. You may also submit by email to clinical.privileges@msjmc.org, by hand delivery to the Information Desk at the hospital, or by mail to the Office of the Medical Director, Sir Lester Bird Medical Centre, Michael’s Mount, P.O. Box 559, St. John’s, Antigua & Barbuda, W.I.
Documents and requirements
How many reference letters are required? +
Three (3) reference letters are required. Letters must not be older than 3 months at the time of submission and must be signed. Unsigned reference letters will not be accepted under any circumstances.
Can I submit documents in a language other than English? +
All submitted documents must be in English. If your original documents are in another language, you must submit both the original and a certified English translation.
Do my transcripts need to be sent by my medical school? +
Yes. Transcripts must be sent directly from your medical school to SLBMC by email or mail. The only exception is if the transcripts are duly notarised, in which case you may include them in your application package.
What does “originals seen” mean on certified documents? +
When a notary or authorised person certifies a copy of your diploma, certificate, or transcript, they must write or stamp “originals seen” on the copy to confirm they compared it to the original. Copies without this notation will not be accepted.
Who should the reference letters be addressed to? +
Reference letters must be addressed to the Medical Director, Sir Lester Bird Medical Centre, Michael’s Mount, P.O. Box 559, St. John’s, Antigua & Barbuda, W.I.
What if I don’t have CAMC 1? +
USMLE Steps 1 & 2 or PLAB 1 & 2 are accepted equivalents. If you hold none of these, you may still apply — however, applicants with a board certifying examination will have an advantage over those who do not.
Conditions of the programme
How long is the internship? +
The internship is a one-year, full-time programme. Interns function as full hospital physicians, taking calls on a one-in-four basis.
Is this a paid internship? +
Yes, this is a paid internship. Details will be discussed with you during the interview.
Will I need a work permit? +
If a work permit is required, support will be provided to assist you with your application to work legally in Antigua.
What is a CARICOM Skills Certificate? +
A CARICOM Skills Certificate is an official document that enables qualified nationals of CARICOM Member States to live and work across the Community without the need for a work permit. If you are a national or citizen of a CARICOM Member State, we strongly encourage you to obtain one prior to commencing the programme. Further information is available through your country’s relevant labour or immigration authority.
Will I need a medical licence? +
Yes, you will need a medical licence from Antigua in order to participate in the programme. If accepted, support will be provided for your application to the Antigua & Barbuda Medical Council.
Am I expected to pay taxes and licensing fees? +
Yes, each successful applicant is expected to pay all applicable government statutory taxes and licensing fees.
Will accommodation be provided? +
Accommodation arrangements are the responsibility of the applicant. We are happy to assist where possible by connecting you with contacts who may help you identify suitable options. We encourage you to begin exploring accommodation early once you have been offered a place in the programme.
